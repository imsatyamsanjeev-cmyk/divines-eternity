Refund & Cancellation Policy

Thank you for supporting handcrafted small businesses! Please read our policy on returns, refunds, and cancellations before placing your orders.

1. Order Cancellations

  • Since all our flower bouquets, frames, and hampers are highly customized and handmade from scratch to order, we accept cancellations **only within 24 hours** of order placement.
  • Cancellations requested after 24 hours will not be accepted as we would have already begun sourcing materials or crafting the specific item.
  • To request a cancellation, please drop us a text on our official WhatsApp number: +91 9353652043.

2. Returns & Replacements

  • Due to the custom and highly fragile nature of our products, we **do not accept general returns** or exchanges based on color preference or minor variation.
  • In the rare event that your product is received in a **damaged state** due to transport, please take a video/photo during unpacking and share it with us on WhatsApp within **12 hours of delivery**.
  • If the damage is verified, we will happily provide a free replacement or execute repair corrections for the damaged item.

3. Refund Processing

  • For eligible cancellations, the full advance amount will be refunded.
  • Refunds are credited directly to your original payment source (via Razorpay or bank transfer).
  • Once initiated, refunds typically take **5 to 7 business days** to reflect in your bank account, depending on your banking institution.

Shipping & Delivery Policy

We make every effort to pack and ship your delicate handcrafted treasures with the highest level of protection. Here are our delivery rules.

1. Local Bangalore Deliveries

  • Our studio is based in Bangalore, Karnataka.
  • For local Bangalore orders, we dispatch via instant courier services (like Dunzo, Porter, or local delivery boys).
  • Local shipping fees are calculated dynamically based on real-time distance from our studio to your address.
  • You can select your preferred delivery date in our Custom Hamper Builder, and we will do our best to match your exact schedule.

2. Nationwide shipping

  • For orders outside Bangalore, we ship via registered national parcel partners.
  • Nationwide shipping takes **3 to 5 business days** for transit after dispatch.
  • Please note that custom hamper frames and delicate bouquets require **2-3 days of crafting time** prior to dispatch.
  • Once dispatched, we will share the tracking number/URL with you directly on WhatsApp.

3. Address Errors & Delays

  • Please ensure your delivery address is accurate and complete, including landmarks, to prevent delays.
  • We are not responsible for delays caused by national holidays, severe weather, courier service delays, or if the recipient is unavailable to accept delivery.
